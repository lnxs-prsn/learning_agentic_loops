# Backbrief template (METHOD §6)

Copy into `backbrief.md` (or into a ticket log for small tasks). Own words, not a copy of the brief.

## BB-<n> — to <BR-n / ticket / re-brief RB-n>
date: <read from the clock>
from: <agent>

1. **Intent as I understand it:** <what + why, in my words; and how it serves the aim>
2. **What I will do** (implied tasks, no gaps or overlaps): <...>
3. **My main effort:** <...>, because <...>
4. **What I need / can't do:** "I can do X, but not Y unless <time/resources/decision>." <or: none>
5. **Where I disagree or see risk:** <the brief's implications as I see them; what would break this plan>
6. **Unclear, and I need clarity on:** <or: none>
7. **Situation check:** has anything changed since the brief? <no / yes: ...>
